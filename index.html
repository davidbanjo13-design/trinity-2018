Trinity School Bexley. Year 11. Class of 2018.

It's been eight years... I think it's time

In America, reunions are practically built into the culture, like it's just expected that you'll go back and see everyone. Whereas the UK, that's not really a thing. We leave school and that's kind of just it. No structure, no expectation, no one organising it so it just doesn't happen. People we spent every day with for five years become people we used to know.

Why this matters to me

Secondary was a time when life just felt simple. We were young, energetic and discovering ourselves. I found myself wishing I could experience that feeling again. That's why I built this: A space for us to reconnect, relive the memories that made us who we are and create new ones together. We went from spending everyday with people, to barely recognising each other. We didn't end year 11 on a good path, so let's come together and make this a legacy!

Who it's for?
